Working in Marlboro County

County seat: Bennettsville

Around the county: Lake Paul Wallace Wildlife Management Area, Lake Wallace, Great Pee Dee River, Little Pee Dee River and I-95, US 1, US 15, SC 9, SC 38.

Known for: The Pee Dee Indian Tribe's seat of government has operated on tribal land in the county since 1976.

Homes and buildings: The 2020 Census counted 12,029 housing units in the county; 66.3% of occupied units were owner-occupied.

Weather that hits roofs here: On March 28, 1984, F4 tornadoes touched down near Bennettsville and McColl during the Carolinas tornado outbreak; Bennettsville was among the hardest-hit communities.
